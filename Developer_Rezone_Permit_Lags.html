Development timing

How long developers take to rezone and permit their land

Median years between when a developer acquired a parcel and when it was rezoned or received a building permit, by developer type. Out-of-state developers are the slowest to pull a building permit and sit longest on rezoned land; national builders permit fastest once they move.

Notes: Developer type from the owner-to-operator crosswalk (national/institutional = named builder or SFR operator; out-of-state = business entity with out-of-state mailing; local = in-state business entity). Rezone dates from Davidson Zoning History (Layer 5); building-permit dates from the merged ePermits/permit history (issued date), first event after acquisition. Medians shown; "acquisition to permit" spans all permitted parcels, "rezone to permit" only parcels that were rezoned. Metro Nashville (last accessed 2026).
