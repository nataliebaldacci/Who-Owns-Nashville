Who Owns Nashville — Data

Flattened, parcel-level research datasets for Davidson County, Tennessee · built entirely from primary government records

These are the flattened, analysis-ready tables behind the Who Owns Nashville project. Each links every Davidson County parcel to its full history — ownership, assessment, characteristics, permits, zoning, and landlord registration — compiled from Metro ePermits, the Metro ParcelHistory ownership record, and the Property Assessor. Files are .xz-compressed CSVs (open directly in pandas: pd.read_csv(path) — it auto-detects xz).

Opening the files. These are .xz-compressed CSVs. In Python: import pandas as pd; df = pd.read_csv("ownership_history_flattened.csv.xz") (pandas auto-detects xz). On the command line: xz -d filename.csv.xz. Permit and zoning histories keep the 10 most-recent events per parcel; the n_permits / n_zoneevents columns record the full totals. All data derives from public Davidson County / Metro Nashville government records and is redistributable.
