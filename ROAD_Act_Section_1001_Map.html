The Act bites in two places: § 1001 mapped to the series

21st Century ROAD to Housing Act, H.R. 6644 § 1001 — the provisions each Part answers.

WHO RENTS NASHVILLE?
§ 1001(a)(5) — Purchase

Defines the covered acquisition.

§ 1001(b) — The Prohibition

Bars institutional purchase of single-family homes.

§ 1001(c) — Renter Outreach Resource

Establishes a tenant information resource.

WHO BUILDS NASHVILLE?
§ 1001(a)(2) — Excepted Purchase CARVE-OUT

Exempts building activity from the ban.

§ 1001(a)(2)(A)

Build, renovate, or rental-conversion for sale.

§ 1001(a)(2)(B)

Build-to-rent program.

Renters live inside the ban and its outreach machinery; builders live inside the exception.

The build-to-rent carve-out, § 1001(a)(2)(B), is the seam where the two Parts meet.
