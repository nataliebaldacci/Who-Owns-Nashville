Owner vs Renter by Structure Type

Davidson County — detached, attached, and two-unit homes, with the homeownership rate across each

The same tenure question, split by how many units are in the building. Bars are the count of occupied homes each year (owner + renter); the red line is the homeownership rate (owner share). Notice the gradient: the more units in the structure, the fewer are owner-occupied.

Source. U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey, Table B25032 (Tenure by Units in Structure), Davidson County. Detached & attached: annual 2010–2024; 2-unit: end-year of the 2015–2019 and 2020–2024 5-year releases. Homeownership rate = owner-occupied ÷ (owner + renter) occupied units of that structure type.
