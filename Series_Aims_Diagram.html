The series

Who Owns Nashville? — the aims of the series

A study of institutional players in one county's housing market, built entirely from primary government records. It walks the housing lifecycle backwards and maps each stage onto the 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act.

The organizing idea

The series runs the development lifecycle in reverse and lays it against the statute. The Act's ban lands downstream (buying existing homes to rent); its exceptions bless upstream (build-to-rent, renovate-to-rent); land banking sits in its blind spot; finance cuts across. Walking backwards moves from the players you can see to the ones only primary-source forensics reveal, and it proves the thesis: the law bans the back of the pipeline and leaves the front open, so institutional capital simply moves upstream.

The seam: the statute is precise where the actors are clear (rental acquisition) and goes blind exactly where they go murky (land banking, finance). The vaguer the actor, the weaker the statute, so the backwards walk exposes where the law fails.
