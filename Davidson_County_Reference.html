The definitions this project runs on, with current parcel counts: how to open any deed image from its instrument number, the Assessor's classification and land-use codes, Metro's base zoning districts and overlays, and the demographic sources for the county. Counts are live parcels, dwelling units, and acres. Residential codes are shaded.

Deed & instrument images

Every ownership record carries an instrument number. Two portal endpoints turn that number into the recorded document image. The gis/file.php form takes the full instrument number and needs no year or .tif; the landrecords/view_image.php form is keyed by recording date and file.

Form A — by instrument number (Deeds, Quit Claim Deeds):
https://davidsonportal.com/gis/file.php?file={InstrumentNumber}
Strip the prefix and space from the recorded instrument. No .tif needed.
Deed  DB-20140529 0046200 → file.php?file=201405290046200
Quit Claim Deed  QC-20260202 0006912 → file.php?file=202602020006912
Plat  PL-20260518 0044255 → file.php?file=202605180044255
Form B — Land Records image by recording date (Deeds of Trust, Trustee's Deeds, older records):
https://davidsonportal.com/landrecords/view_image.php?key=/img/tn/davidson/{YYYY}/{MMDD}/{InstrumentNumber}.tif
Deed of Trust  DOT 202404180027955, recorded 4/18/24 → .../2024/0418/0027955.tif
Older record → .../1998/0902/199809021095310.tif

The Instrument and InstrumentType fields in the ownership-history feed carry the prefix (DB = Deed, QC = Quit Claim Deed, DOT = Deed of Trust, PL = Plat) that tells you which document you are opening.

Zoning overlays

Overlay districts sit on top of the base zone and change what is allowed. DDU (detached accessory dwelling unit) and RAS (residential accessory structure) govern backyard units; UZO (urban zoning overlay) removes minimum parking; FLD is the flood overlay. Overlay membership is a separate Planning layer, so per-overlay parcel counts are not carried on the cadastral.

The county-level demographic and housing context is pulled from the sources below, so every number stays replayable.

  • Tennessee community profile (Davidson County): tn.gov/transparenttn/local/community-profiles.html
  • Homeownership rate, 5-year (Davidson): FRED HOWNRATEACS047037
  • Population (Davidson): FRED TNDAVI5POP

Full ACS housing tables in use: B25002 (occupancy), B25024 (units in structure), B25032 (tenure by structure), B25036/B25037 (tenure by year built), B25077 (median value), B25058 (median rent), B25123 (physical / financial conditions).
