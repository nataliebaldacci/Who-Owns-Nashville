Davidson County Housing Indicators Over Time

2010–2024 · population, homeownership, values, rents, and the growth of the rental stock

Six housing indicators for Nashville–Davidson County, year by year. The through-line: population, home values, rents, and rental units all climbed steeply — while the homeownership rate fell from 60% to 55.5%.

Sources. U.S. Census Bureau ACS 5-Year: B25077 (median value), B25032 (tenure by units), CP04 (median gross rent); FRED TNDAVI5POP (population) and HOWNRATEACS047037 (homeownership rate). Davidson County; median gross rent available 2015–2024.
