Owner vs Renter of One-Unit Homes — Davidson County, 2010–2024

The renter share of Nashville's one-unit homes, year by year

Each column is one year. Together they show the share of Davidson County's occupied one-unit homes (detached + attached) held by owner-occupants vs. renters. Hover any year for the counts.

Owner-occupiedRenter-occupied
The shift. The renter share of one-unit homes climbed from 16.5% in 2010 to a peak of 21.1% in 2014, then settled near 19–20% — leaving Davidson's one-unit rental share persistently above the national ~16%. In counts, renter-occupied one-unit homes grew from 26,485 to 38,835.

Source. U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey, Table B25032 (Tenure by Units in Structure), Davidson County. One-unit = detached + attached; owner/renter of occupied one-unit properties, 2010–2024.
