The number that grew. Renter-occupied one-unit homes in Davidson County rose from 26,485 in 2010 to 38,835 in 2024 — an increase of 12,350 homes (+47%), far outpacing the growth in owner-occupied one-unit homes (+18%) over the same period.

Source. U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey, Table B25032 (Tenure by Units in Structure), Davidson County. One-unit = detached + attached; occupied one-unit properties, 2010–2024.
