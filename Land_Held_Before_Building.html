Land banking

How long land sat vacant before it was built on

For every home in Davidson County, the years the owner held the lot as vacant land before construction, reconstructed from the chain of title and the year built. The pattern flips the usual assumption: individuals sit on lots longest, national builders build within a year, and hold times are lengthening for everyone since 2020.

Ownership from Davidson County Parcel History MapServer Layer 2 with the owner-to-operator crosswalk; year built from the assessor build record (last accessed 2026). Owner type: named operator via the crosswalk (mostly national homebuilders plus AMH build-to-rent), any other business entity, or individual. Recent build years have thinner samples.
