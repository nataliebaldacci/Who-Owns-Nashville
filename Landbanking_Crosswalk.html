Land-Bank Option Agreements

Each row is a recorded land-bank deal: a capital provider holds the land (the "Owner" / "Land Bank"), a homebuilder holds a recorded option to buy the finished lots on a takedown schedule. The land bank carries the dirt and the carrying cost; the builder draws lots down over 1–4 years and builds. Every instrument links to the recorded image at the Davidson County Register of Deeds. ← back to site

How to read the takedown. A land bank's parcel count falls as the builder exercises the option lot by lot. DRP TN 1 shows 0 because Beazer has drawn down all of Cameron Park; Millrose and DRP Multistate 1 show 1 because their takedowns are nearly complete. KLLB (131) and TPG MTH Zermatt (133) are early in their schedules. The structure separates the party that holds the land from the party that builds on it: the land bank warehouses entitled lots and meters them out over a multi-year term, marked up at each takedown.
