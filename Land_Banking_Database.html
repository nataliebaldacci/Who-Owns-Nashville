Land-Banking Deals

How builders bank their land

A land banker buys the dirt and holds title; the builder signs a recorded option and draws lots one takedown at a time, paying as it builds. The builder keeps the land off its balance sheet, but pays the banker's return on top of the land cost, so the finished lot costs more. Every instrument below is a public Davidson County recording.
