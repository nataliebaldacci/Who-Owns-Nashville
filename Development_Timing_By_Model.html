Development timing by model: build-to-rent vs merchant builder vs lot developer vs landbanker

Median years each development model takes to rezone, permit, and build, and how often it ever builds at all. Speculative landbankers sit on rezoned land 12.5 years before permitting and build only 24% of what they buy; build-to-rent operators move fastest.

Notes: Entities classified per the project BTR/Landbanker/Developer roster and owner-to-operator crosswalk. Lags = median years from acquisition, using Davidson zoning history (Layer 5) and permit history (issued date); build year from the assessor. n=36,114 parcels (28,156 merchant/captive builder, 3,064 build-to-rent, 2,706 lot developer, 2,188 speculative landbanker). Metro Nashville (last accessed 2026).
