★ Development Timing: BTR vs Builder vs Landbanker

How long each development model takes to rezone, permit, and build, and how often it builds at all. Speculative landbankers sit on rezoned land 12.5 years and build just 24%; build-to-rent moves fastest. Time-to-develop and do-they-build views.

★ How Long Developers Take to Rezone & Permit

Median years from acquisition to rezoning and to building permit, comparing national/institutional builders, local builders, and out-of-state developers. Out-of-state developers are slowest to permit (3.5 yr) and sit longest on rezoned land (6 yr); national builders permit fastest.

★ Who Sits on Vacant Land, and How Long

Every vacant parcel by years the current owner has held it undeveloped. Individuals hold a median 9 years (25% held 20+ years); corporate holders are mostly homebuilders' short-term pipelines (median 4 years). By-duration and by-operator views.

★ How Long Land Sat Before It Was Built
