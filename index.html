The flagship, cross-cutting tools: the full searchable map, the narrated scroll story, and the primary-source databases.

★ Full Parcel Map — Search Every Home

The complete interactive map. Search any owner, address, ZIP, or APN; every corporate-owned home is a house glyph colored by operator, with census-tract, council-district, and ZIP overlays. Click any parcel for the full county toolkit.

★ A Decade of Institutional Acquisition (Scroll Story)

Scroll through the narrated story — watch corporate buyers sweep across Davidson County year by year, the map building under the text as you read.

★ Parcel Data Explorer

The full flattened record for every parcel. Browse and sort the table, filter by owner/address/APN, then click any row for that parcel's whole history — chain of title, permits, and zoning.

★ Document Database

Every primary source, organized by Part — property cards, ownership deeds, deeds of trust, trustees' deeds, permit receipts, building permits, and Metro zoning/legislation links.

★ Parcel Address Search (with photos)

Type any Davidson County address and see the assessor's photo of the house plus its parcel record — owner, APN, and direct county links. 198,358 parcels.

★ How the Data Was Filtered

The filter funnel: from all 286,721 county parcels down to the 205,934-home research scope, the 16,616 corporate-owned homes, and the 4,264 held by named institutional operators. Every stage links to a live, replayable county query.

The year-by-year shift in Nashville's one-unit stock — the renter share climbing from 16.5% to a 21.1% peak, then settling near 19–20%, persistently above the national ~16%.
