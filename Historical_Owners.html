Who has ever owned Nashville

Corporate ownership is measured two ways, from two primary datasets, because “who owns it now” and “who has ever owned it” are different questions. This page is the second framework: the full transaction history, every entity that ever took title.

Current owners — the parcel framework
One row per parcel, today’s owner. The 205,933 one- and two-unit homes that fit the 21st Century Housing Act, filtered to the corporate owners holding them right now. Cadastral, MapServer/4.

One row per deed event. Every entity that ever took title, not just the current holder, so a corporate investor who held and sold a home years ago still shows up. Ownership History, MapServer/2.

The dataset

The current cadastral shows only the present owner, so it cannot say who held a home as an investor five or ten years ago. For that we read the Ownership History (ParcelHistory, MapServer/2): 1,641,434 recorded deed events, one per transfer, each carrying the owner name, mailing address, and state at the time, the acquisition date, and the recorded instrument. Running the same corporate test over the deed record surfaces every entity that ever took title.

Who counts as a corporate owner

A recorded owner is treated as a corporate entity when its name carries a business token and is not a government body or a family trust:

(Name LIKE '%LLC%' OR '%LLP%' OR '% LP%' OR '%L.P%' OR '% INC%' OR '%CORP%' OR '%LTD%' OR '%LIMITED%' OR '% CO %' OR '%CO.%' OR '%COMPANY%' OR '%PARTNER%' OR '%ASSOC%' OR '%HOMES%' OR '%HOLDING%' OR '%CAPITAL%' OR '%INVEST%' OR '%FUND%' OR '%SOLUTION%' OR '%SFR%' OR '%ASSET%' OR '%BORROW%' OR '% JV%') minus government (CITY OF, HOUSING AUTH, METRO...GOV, STATE OF, COUNTY OF, HUD, MDHA, UNITED STATES) minus trusts (TRUST, REVOCABLE, LIVING TR, FAMILY TR)

Out of state is StateCode <> 'TN'. Out of county is a mailing ZIP outside the Davidson County set (37013, 37072, 37076, 37080, 37115, 37138, 37189, and 37201–37221, 37228). Grouping the corporate deed events by owner name and keeping those with more than a couple of transfers ranks operators by how active they have been across the entire history, not just their current book.
