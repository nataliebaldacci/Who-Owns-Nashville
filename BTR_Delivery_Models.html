The Four Models of Build-to-Rent

Following the Urban Institute's framework (Goodman & Zinn, 2023), every institutional build-to-rent operator in Nashville sorts into four delivery models. The test that separates them is who owns the land during construction. Scattered-site acquisition of existing homes is a separate, non-BTR channel. 3,317 BTR parcels.
