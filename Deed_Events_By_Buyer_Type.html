Davidson County deed events by buyer type

Every recorded ownership transfer in Davidson County, classified by who received the deed. Click a buyer type in the legend to toggle it. Switch between counts and share, or view the full record back to 1969.

Notes: Author's classification of 1.55 million Davidson County ownership-transfer events, parsed from the Davidson County Parcel History MapServer Layer 2 (Ownership History), maps.nashville.gov/arcgis2/rest/services/Parcels/ParcelHistory/MapServer/2 (last accessed May 13, 2026). Each event is classified by buyer-name pattern: SFR REIT (named parent brands: Progress, AMH, Amherst, Invitation, Tricon, FirstKey, KKR/MCH); iBuyer (Opendoor, Offerpad, Homeward, Zillow Homes); Homebuilder (NVR, Beazer, Pulte, D.R. Horton, Lennar, Meritage, Ole South, Regent and similar); Bank/REO (GSE and major bank trustees); Trust/estate (revocable/family/living-trust naming); Other entity (LLC/Inc./Corp./LP without an operator match); Individual (natural-person grantee). 2026 is a partial year.
