Housing supply

Where prices rose and where building kept up, by census tract

Each dot is a Davidson County census tract: horizontal is how fast it added homes (construction rate), vertical is how much home prices grew. Tracts that built little but saw prices soar sit top-left. Color shows the institutional-ownership share. Hover any tract.

Notes: Tract construction rate (permits per residential parcel) and price growth (early vs late median sale) from Davidson County Parcel History and permit records; institutional share from the owner-to-operator classification. A Glaeser-Gyourko-style read of local supply elasticity. Metro Nashville Cadastral + Parcel History (last accessed May 22, 2026).
