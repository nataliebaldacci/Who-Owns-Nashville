Top Institutional Owners of Single-Family Homes

← Back to Who Owns Nashville
The four largest institutional owners of single-family homes in Davidson County are the Big 4 SFR REITs. Progress Residential holds 1,220 parcels, American Homes 4 Rent 1,182, and Amherst Residential 699. Invitation Homes has fallen to just 108 after selling roughly 708 Nashville homes to Tricon in 2019. Landlord-registration compliance splits sharply: the established REITs register most of their homes (Progress 79%, Amherst 88%), while newer and iBuyer entrants barely register at all (VineBrook 0.9%, Rithm 9%).
