Reading the dollar figures: each Wall Street pool deed of trust is re-recorded against every parcel in the pool, each recording stating the full pool amount. Recording-level totals therefore overstate distinct debt many times over. Counts and dollar sums here are recording-level (one row = one parcel-level filing); true facility sizes live in the SEC prospectuses on EDGAR.
Davidson County commercial deed-of-trust recordings ≥ $10 million reveal a small, concentrated institutional capital structure financing Nashville single-family rental. Seven Wall Street investment banks — Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, Barclays, Royal Bank of Canada, German American Capital Corp (Deutsche Bank), Wells Fargo, and Nomura — account for the majority of recorded institutional debt on Davidson SFR collateral. On the equity side, roughly fifteen operators (Pretium, Blackstone, Cerberus/FirstKey, Amherst, KKR, Apollo, Rithm, Starwood SFR JV, Bridge, Invitation Homes, American Homes 4 Rent) hold the parcels serving as pool collateral. This is not diversified ownership. It is a concentrated capital structure with Nashville housing as collateral, managed from New York, Toronto, London, Frankfurt, Tokyo, and Los Angeles.
