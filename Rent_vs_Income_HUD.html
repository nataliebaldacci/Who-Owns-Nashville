Rents rose faster than incomes

The lines below show the rent against what a household at each HUD income limit can pay at the 30%-of-income standard — the extremely-low-income line never comes close, and the gap widens every year.

Method & sources

Housing is affordable at the 30% standard when rent takes no more than 30% of income. Two quantities follow: the income a rent requires, rent × 12 / 0.30 (the 2026 2BR FMR of $1,730 requires $69,200), and the rent an income affords, income × 0.30 / 12 (the 2026 extremely-low-income limit of $34,850 affords $871). The shortfall against the FMR is $859 a month; paying it anyway would take 60% of that household's income. HUD reports the two-bedroom FMR and the four-person income limit as the standard pairing for a modest family unit.
