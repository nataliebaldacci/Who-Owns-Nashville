Methodology

How the data was filtered

The research universe is built in three steps from the full Metro Nashville assessor cadastral. Each step narrows toward the single-family homes the 21st Century Housing Act protects, and each surviving set is broken down by land use, zoning, and feature type, with parcels, dwelling units, acres, and assessed value.

The research universe. Land use is single family, zero-lot-line, duplex, or residential condo (or unclassified); feature type is a real dwelling, not a condo shell, common area, or open space; mobile / manufactured homes excluded per § 1001(a)(3)(B). This is a “single-family home” under the Act: a structure with two or fewer dwelling units.
