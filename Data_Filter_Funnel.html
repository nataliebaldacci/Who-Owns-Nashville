How the data was filtered

The research universe is built from the full Metro Nashville assessor cadastral, narrowed to the single-family homes the 21st Century Housing Act protects, then split by who owns them. Each step is broken down six ways — land use, zoning, feature type, structure type, dwelling-unit count, and year built — with parcels, dwelling units, acres, and assessed value. Click a tab to switch the breakdown.
